10 new words

1. tool a thing that helps you do work
2. summary a short text with the main ideas
3. note a short record of important facts
4. flashcard a card with a word and its meaning
5. mistake something you do wrong
6. checklist a list of things to do
7. schedule a plan of your time
8. focus to think about one thing only
9. translate to say words in another language
10. useful very helpful
